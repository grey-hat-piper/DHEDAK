About DHEDAK

From information to impact.

Health information creates impact when people can understand it, find the right service and access support. DHEDAK aims to close that gap, with an architecture ready for more African countries.

  1. 01

    Educate

    Make reliable health information easier to understand.

  2. 02

    Connect

    Help people find verified healthcare services.

  3. 03

    Resource

    Strengthen links between providers and suppliers.

  4. 04

    Track & improve

    Organise data, monitor programmes and learn from results.
